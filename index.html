Published Research Paper (Collaborative Work) Armenian Scientific Contribution

Investigating Ground State Properties of Lithium Hydride (LiH) Using Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE)

Abstract Summary: Simulating molecular systems at the quantum level is essential for breakthroughs in clean energy storage, materials science, and pharmaceuticals. Traditional computers face massive scaling roadblocks because quantum states grow exponentially with system size.

To bypass these limits, our research applies the Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) to analyze the ground state properties of Lithium Hydride (LiH). VQE establishes a cooperative loop between quantum hardware and classical optimization: the quantum processor evaluates trial energy states, while the classical optimizer refines parameters to locate absolute ground state energy configurations.

As a fundamental four-electron molecule, LiH offers a pristine testing ground for early-stage quantum hardware, bridging light hydrogen dynamics with heavier atomic structures to pave the path toward scalable quantum simulations.
